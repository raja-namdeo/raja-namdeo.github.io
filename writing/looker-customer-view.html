One Looker view for Sales and Legal

Sales and Legal did not need another chart. They needed the same picture of a customer. I designed Looker dashboards for account and API usage so both teams worked from that picture.

The data was account and API usage. The audience was not the engineering team that already had logs. Sales used it for the customer conversation. Legal used it for the same account, without a second export.

What I treated as the job

  • Looker dashboards for account usage and API usage.
  • One customer view, shared by Sales and Legal.
  • The dashboard as part of the platform work, next to the Admin Center framework, not a side report.

Cross-functional collaboration here was the requirement, not a soft skill on the side. If the two teams could not open the same view, the dashboard had failed.
